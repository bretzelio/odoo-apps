Product Cost Without Tax Display

See your real product costs at a glance, taxes excluded

This module enhances the product form by displaying the cost price excluding supplier taxes right next to the standard cost field. It mirrors the built-in behavior already available for sale prices, bringing the same convenience to the purchasing side.

Key Features

✓ Tax-excluded cost display — Shows "(= X,XX Excl. Taxes)" next to the supplier taxes field when taxes apply, formatted in your company currency. Translated to your language (e.g. "hors taxes" in French)
✓ Automatic computation — Recalculates instantly when cost or supplier taxes change
✓ Currency-aware formatting — Uses the company currency with proper locale formatting
✓ Smart visibility — Only appears when taxes actually change the cost (hidden when cost equals cost excl. taxes)

How It Works

  1. Configure your accounting to display prices tax included (Accounting → Settings → Default Taxes → Included in Price)
  2. Install the module from the Apps menu
  3. Set supplier taxes on your products (General Information tab)
  4. The cost excluding taxes appears automatically on the product form, right next to the cost field
